FINN design options

Five directions, all in liquid glass with rounded corners. Each shows the Home feed on a laptop (two panes: the list, and the open alert) and on a phone (the feed, then the alert as a full sheet). Real data: the 7-day feed and the OLAELEC results alert as of 14 Nov, 23:23 IST. The price line in the chart is illustrative; its end values are real.

AHighlights

Annual-report highlights

Milk-glass panes over a deep cobalt cover plate, the concentric rule of an annual-report cover behind them. The alert opens on a highlights spread: the quarter's three figures set large, like page two of a report.

Schibsted Grotesk · Spline Sans Mono

Risk: The cobalt is loud for something opened every day; it should be checked on a long session.

BPink Sheet

The financial pink paper

Warm glass over salmon newsprint with a faint fibre. The tier bar is the navigation: Alerts 13 · Minor 7 · Hidden 20, each segment as wide as its count, so the filter's work is visible before anything is read.

Onest · Red Hat Mono

Risk: Familiar: every reader of the pink papers knows this ground. That is both the appeal and the risk.

CMagnitude

Star-atlas chart (dark)

Night-navy glass over a faint star field. Importance is dot size, as on a star chart: alerts are large red-amber points, minor items small, hidden filings faint. A sky strip plots the whole 7-day window by time.

Hanken Grotesk · Azeret Mono

Risk: Dark only; dense tables in a bright office read less well than on light panes.

Open Magnitude full size

DSection Boards

Reference-manual tab boards

Milk acetate glass over a full-strength chrome-yellow board. Each section owns a hue (Feed yellow, Search ultramarine, Portfolio grass, Rules oxide), shown as coloured tabs on the right edge; the current one is the board.

Mona Sans · Fragment Mono

Risk: The strongest colour of the five; least like a typical finance app.

ELiquid

Category standard, played straight

Clear liquid glass with a soft colour wallpaper behind it and white, specular panes, close to Apple's current system look. Neutral ink, one blue accent.

Geist · Geist Mono

Risk: The most familiar option; it reads as modern, but other apps look like it too.
